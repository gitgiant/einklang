# [lab] [done] Owner voting, live pruning, feedback digest

Gallery 👍/👎 with reason tags. Live: `+`/`-` keys and a Select+△ / Select+✕ pad chord on the owner's pad, a toast on all hosts, and a 👎 that skips to the next scene at the next boundary. The server appends to `data/votes.jsonl`. Score = ups − downs: it weights rotation, a 👎 halves the weight, and net ≤ −2 retires the scene. `einklang-lab feedback` writes `lab/feedback.json` and `lab/FEEDBACK.md` with per scene/piece/vendor scores and updates scene status. Full spec: docs/prd-lab.md.
