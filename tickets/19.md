# [v1] Scene transition framework: 14 synced, beat-length effects

Server-scheduled transitions on scene switches. The schedule carries `{type, ticks, seed}`, starts on the beat or bar boundary, and queues an overlapping request for after the running one. Both scenes render live into HDR targets and a transition pass mixes them before the post chain, whose settings interpolate. Effects: cut, blend, flash, wipe, iris, dissolve, luma, zoom, glitch, stutter, swirl, melt, audio mask, pixelate, each with its own progress curve. The panel picks transition + length (beats), and random is resolved by the server. Gallery auto-cycle preview. Full spec: docs/prd-transitions.md.
